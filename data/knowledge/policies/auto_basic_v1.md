# Demo AUTO_BASIC policy

DEMO / SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL INSURANCE CONTRACT

This separate invented product is a control for product filtering. Its rules are synthetic and do not represent a real insurer or French insurance law.

## Collision Damage

AUTO_BASIC covers accidental collision repairs only to front and rear bumpers. Headlights, doors, and body panels are outside the described collision cover for this demo product.

## Deductible

The AUTO_BASIC demo deductible is EUR 600 per incident. The demo limit is EUR 5,000 of eligible bumper repairs before applying this deductible.

## Supporting Evidence

Provide a completed claim form, an account of the collision, photographs of the damaged bumper, and an itemized garage repair estimate. Intentional damage and mechanical wear are excluded from this demo cover.
