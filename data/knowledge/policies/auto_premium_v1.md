# Demo AUTO_PREMIUM policy

DEMO / SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL INSURANCE CONTRACT

These invented terms demonstrate retrieval architecture. They are not French insurance rules, legal guidance, or a promise of actual insurance coverage. Only the listed demonstration topics are defined here.

## Collision Damage

AUTO_PREMIUM covers accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle resulting from a collision with another vehicle or a fixed object. A collision while parking is included. Coverage applies to repair of collision damage, subject to the deductible, covered parts, and exclusions in this demo policy.

## Covered Vehicle Parts

Covered collision repairs include the front bumper, rear bumper, headlights, doors, and body panels. Damage to a covered part must result from the reported collision. Mechanical wear and routine maintenance are outside this collision cover.

## Deductible

The demo collision deductible is EUR 300 per incident. It is the portion of an eligible repair cost borne by the policyholder. For an eligible EUR 1,000 repair, the demo reimbursement is EUR 700. If the eligible repair cost is below EUR 300, no repair reimbursement is payable under this demo calculation.

## Exclusions and Limits

Intentional damage, mechanical wear, and damage occurring during an organized motor race are excluded. The demo limit for eligible collision repair is EUR 12,000 per incident before applying the deductible. Do not infer coverage for events that this document does not describe.

## Supporting Evidence

A collision claim needs a completed claim form, an account of the collision, and an itemized garage repair estimate. For claimed headlight or body panel damage, provide photographs of the affected parts. If third-party involvement is reported, include an accident report describing that involvement. Missing evidence does not itself establish an exclusion; the handling procedure explains follow-up.
